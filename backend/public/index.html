Features

Built to stay connected

Everything you need from a VPN, and nothing to configure.

Instant failover

Backup servers in your chosen location stay ready. If the one you use drops, traffic moves to the next within a second and your location does not change.

Encrypted by default

Your traffic is encrypted to the server. Plain, unencrypted connections, which anyone along the way could read, are blocked unless you allow them.

Many locations

Choose a country, or let the app pick the fastest. You see how many servers are available and their ping before you connect.

Local sites stay local

Pick a region and its sites open directly, at full speed, instead of going through the VPN.

FAQ

Questions

Is my traffic private?

Your traffic is encrypted between your device and the server, so your network or internet provider cannot see what you open. Sites that use HTTPS stay encrypted all the way to the site. Like with any VPN, the server you connect through can see which sites you reach and how much data you transfer, so a VPN does not make you anonymous.

What do you store about me?

Your username, a salted hash of your password, and counters of the data and time you have used. The app does not send the sites you visit. See the privacy page.

Why was I disconnected?

Each account can have a data allowance, a time allowance and an end date. When one is reached the app disconnects and says so. Your account page shows what is left and when it renews.

Can I use one account on several devices?

Yes. Usage from all your devices counts towards the same allowance.

I forgot my password.

Contact the person who gave you the account, or write to us using the address below. Passwords are never stored in a form we can read, so we reset them rather than recover them.
