Privacy

How Albatross handles your information, in plain words.

What your account stores

  • Your username, and your password as a salted one-way hash. We cannot read your password.
  • Counters: how much data you have used and how long you have been connected, for the current period and in total, and when you were last seen.
  • The limits set on your account: data and time allowance, renewal period and end date.

There is no email address, name or phone number. Because of that, a lost password cannot be recovered; it can only be reset.

What the app sends us

Only your username and password when you sign in, and numbers of bytes and seconds when it reports usage. It does not send the sites you visit, the addresses you connect to, or the content of your traffic.

Learning from other users (only if you turn it on)

With Learn from other users on, the app sends a few times a day how many tests of each kind of server passed on your network (for example "Reality on port 443: 40 of 50"), under your provider's network number (ASN). It sends no server addresses, no address of your device, no username and no install number. Totals are kept for two weeks, and a network's totals are shown to apps only after several reports. Reports are counted per one-way tag of your network address, only to limit how many one device can send, and that count is forgotten after a day.

The support chat

The chat on our websites is answered by an AI assistant that runs on Cloudflare. What you write in it goes to the account service and to that model, and nowhere else; your browser keeps the conversation only for this tab. When the chat ends, a short summary and the conversation are emailed to our team, with the page you were on, your device type, your country and the way to reach you if you left one, so that we can follow up. Nothing from the chat is kept on our servers after that. Do not write passwords or personal details in it.

Our Telegram bot works the same way, with one difference: Telegram delivers each message on its own, so the bot keeps a chat's messages, with your Telegram name, username and number (never your phone number), until about half an hour after your last message, and a ticket's messages until it is closed (a ticket nobody writes to for three days is closed). Then the conversation, with a summary, is emailed to our team and the messages are deleted; a closed ticket keeps only its number. If you rate our help, only the total is counted. The limits that stop abuse remember a one-way tag of your number, not the number, for up to a day. The language you pick in the bot is remembered for a year under the same kind of tag, apart from your chat, so that you are not asked again. If we block someone we keep their number alone, so that the block holds.

Buying data with Telegram Stars

If you buy data in our Telegram bot, Telegram takes the payment in Stars; we never see your payment details. Telegram tells us that the payment was made, with your Telegram number and the payment's id. We keep that, the package and the account it went to for two years, so that a payment can be refunded and our accounts are in order. An account bought in the bot remembers its buyer's Telegram number for as long as the account exists, so that only that Telegram account can ask the bot for a new password; deleting the account deletes it. A package nobody paid for is forgotten after a day.

What we do not store

This service does not record the sites you visit or the content of your connections. Sign-in attempts are counted per username and per a one-way tag of your network address, only to slow down password guessing, and are forgotten after a short while.

What the servers can see

Your connection is carried through servers that may be run by independent parties. Treat any VPN server as able to see which sites you connect to and how much data you transfer. Content of sites that use HTTPS stays encrypted from your device to the site. Connections that are not encrypted can be read by whoever carries them, so the app blocks them by default.

A VPN protects you from your local network and provider. It does not make you anonymous.

Cookies and tracking

The website keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. It uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

Ask the person who runs this service to delete your account. It removes everything listed above.
