    Servers not updating, or this page hard to reach?

    On some networks the operator's DNS answers for our addresses with a wrong address, so the page and the servers in your connection app stop loading. Encrypted DNS goes around that. On iPhone and iPad, install one of these profiles in Safari (Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, then Install); on Android, set Private DNS to dns.google; the Albatross app for Windows and Android needs nothing.

    Encrypted DNS: Google Encrypted DNS: Cloudflare

    iPhone, iPad and Mac

    On Apple devices a connection app from the App Store carries the traffic. Pick yours, then add your servers to it with one tap; they appear under the Albatross name and refresh by themselves.

    Keep this page handy: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app.
