Telegram support bot

In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, pick a name and a username that ends in bot, and paste the token it gives you here. The bot first asks each person for a language (Persian, English, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Turkish, Urdu, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish or French) and then answers in it, with a menu, guides and an assistant; people reach you as tickets, which you answer by replying in the team chat (the card names their language).

Selling data with Telegram Stars

The Telegram bot sells data packages for Stars (Telegram requires Stars for digital goods), opens the account and sends its username and password in the chat. Prices follow from what 1 GB costs and from the exchange rates below; change a rate and the Stars follow. Telegram pays out about $13 per 1,000 Stars, after 21 days, from 1,000 Stars up (through Fragment, in TON). Try a payment first: in the team's chat send /teststars (1 Star), then /refund it.

Buyers pick a package or build their own plan in the bot: 5 to 500 GB a month, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. A plan gives all its data at once and lasts its months; the price follows from the GB price, less the discount for the data a month and the one for the months (empty: none).
